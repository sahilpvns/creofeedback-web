Last Updated: August 17, 2026

CreoFeedback may use Google Sign-In to authenticate users. We only request the information necessary to create and manage your CreoFeedback account.

If you connect a YouTube channel, CreoFeedback may process authorized channel and content information to provide channel analysis, reports and AI-powered recommendations.

Information required to generate AI-powered reports and recommendations may be processed by our AI service providers. We use such processing to provide features including channel analysis, content suggestions and AI coaching.

Feedback submitted through CreoFeedback may be shown to eligible users or viewers for the purpose of providing creator feedback. We may use automated systems to help maintain feedback quality and platform integrity.

CreoFeedback may maintain information related to points earned, spent or purchased by users to operate the platform's points economy.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

CreoFeedback may integrate with third-party services such as Google, YouTube, payment providers, analytics providers and AI service providers. Their respective privacy policies may also apply.

10. Children's Privacy

CreoFeedback is not intended for children under the minimum age permitted by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited.

11. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal information.
