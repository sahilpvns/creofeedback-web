Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

Our Commitment

CreoFeedback is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We maintain a zero-tolerance policy towards any content or behavior that exploits or endangers children.

Prevention Measures

  1. Age Restriction: Users must be 18+ years to use our app (verified via Google Sign-In)
  2. AI Content Moderation: All user-generated content is screened by AI for inappropriate material
  3. User Reporting System: In-app reporting for any suspicious content or behavior
  4. Account Termination: Immediate ban for users violating child safety policies
  5. Data Security: Encrypted storage and transmission of all user data

Reporting CSAE
