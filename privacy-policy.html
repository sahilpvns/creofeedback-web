4. Points Economy

CreoFeedback operates on a points-based system that allows users to earn and spend points for various activities within the App.

4.1 Earning Points

Users may earn points by participating in activities such as:

  • Providing feedback on tasks
  • Watching videos in the Video Booster feed
  • Testing apps
  • Referring new users
  • Completing daily streaks

4.2 Spending Points

Points may be used within the App to:

  • Create tasks and receive feedback
  • Boost videos
  • Access premium features

4.3 Purchasing Points

Users may purchase points through Google Play's in-app billing system. All point purchases are final and non-refundable, except where required by applicable law or Google Play's refund policies.

Points have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for real currency outside of the App.
